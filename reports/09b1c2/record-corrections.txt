2. THE PAPER-BAND GROUND HAS A FOURTH DELTA, AND IT IS 1294
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
`e837e2b` quantified two of the four grounds a control can sit on and left the
third and fourth unstated:

    paper ROOT ghost   rgb(181,181,176) -> rgb(125,126,124)   delta 1527  COUNTED
    graphite ghost     rgb( 69, 73, 72) -> rgb(104,108,106)   delta  615  not counted

The paper BAND nested in a graphite root — the ground that falsified two 09a
proposals and has its own `--sf-control-rule` binding — was never given a
number. It is:

    --sf-rule          rgba(18,23,25,.30) over --tl-paper #eee9de -> rgb(172,170,163)
    --sf-control-rule  rgba(18,23,25,.54) over --tl-paper #eee9de -> rgb(119,120,116)

    dY 50.55   dI 2.76   dQ -0.30
    delta = 0.5053(50.55)^2 + 0.299(2.76)^2 + 0.1957(0.30)^2 = 1293.5  ≈ 1294

Computed independently here and it agrees with QA's figure to the unit. It sits
BELOW the 1409 cutoff, which is why every crop containing a paper-band ghost —
the `inner-next-*` set and part of `inner-hero-*` — changed without failing.
That is the fourth ground's contribution to the seventeen, and it was the
missing quarter of an adjudication that claimed to cover all four.


3. THE PIXELMATCH CUTOFF IS `35215 * threshold^2`, NOT `35215 * threshold`
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Commit `86a3b70`, THIS packet, stated:

    "pixelmatch counts a pixel only when its YIQ delta exceeds
     `threshold * 35215`   ...   cutoff at 0.2   7 043.0"

WRONG, by a factor of five. Read from the installed comparator,
`playwright-core/lib/third_party/pixelmatch.js:64`:

    const maxDelta = 35215 * options.threshold * options.threshold;

    correct cutoff at threshold 0.2 = 35215 x 0.04 = 1408.6

Two things follow, one of which survives and one of which does not.

  SURVIVES — the conclusion about `.tl-cad-drop`. Its delta is 752.1, still
  below 1409, so the 708 changed pixels are still counted as ZERO and
  `maxDiffPixels: 200` is still never reached. The landing golden legitimately
  passed and the reason is still the per-pixel threshold rather than the
  budget.

  DOES NOT SURVIVE — the size of the blind spot I claimed. "Every golden in
  this repo is blind to any colour change under 7 043" is wrong; the real
  figure is 1 409, which is a much narrower band. The claim was five times too
  alarming.

  Also corrected: the break-even threshold. It is `sqrt(delta / 35215)`, not
  `delta / 35215`, so this change would begin to count below threshold 0.146
  and not 0.0214.

The blind spot is still real and still worth the next phase's attention — 1409
is wide enough to have hidden the 752 of this fix, the 615 of C1's graphite
ghosts and the 1294 of C1's paper-band ghosts, all of which are contrast
changes to a control boundary. But it is 1409 wide, not 7043.

`e837e2b` had this right — "the comparator's default cutoff of 1409" — and
this packet contradicted a correct earlier figure with an incorrect new one.
