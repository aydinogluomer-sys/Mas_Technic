test(qa): round 3 step 2 — the guard that would have caught R2-1, and it caught a second one on its first run

`e2e/qa-p08-scroll-region-reach.spec.ts`. Six tests, three lanes
(mobile-320, mobile-375, desktop-1280), 16 tables over 8 of 22 public routes,
64 header cells.

IT IS NOT BUILT ON THE PREDICATE THE PACKET PROPOSED, and step 1 is why:
`scrollWidth <= clientWidth + 1 || isScrollable(el)` measured TRUE on R2-1 at
320, 375 and 390 in a live A/B, because the pre-fix scroll region FIT ITS OWN
BOX (584 content in a 584 box) and was simply 627.875px wide in a 375px
viewport. The failure is one level above where that expression looks.

So it walks every `<table>` — not a class; a class-keyed walk cannot see a
table whose wrapper changed, and the landing's `.tl-nexus-table-wrap` is
already a second wrapper — finds its nearest scrolling ancestor, and asks four
questions in order: does the table already fit the viewport; is the CONTAINER'S
OWN BOX inside the viewport (this is the R2-1 rule); driven to maximum scroll
does the table's right edge come inside; and can a keyboard reach it. Then, as
a separate test, the symptom: can every header cell's start AND end be brought
inside the viewport.

FOUR CONTROLS. Three feed `unreachableTables()` records it must reject —
R2-1's own measured numbers as a permanent fixture, a clipped overflow, and a
scroller with no focus stop — and one is live: it restores the pre-C4 markup in
the DOM of the real page and requires the guard to go red. That last one is the
one that matters; the other three only prove the comparison is not hollow.

TWO OF MY OWN INSTRUMENT BUGS, FOUND AND FIXED BEFORE THIS WAS TRUSTED:

  · the first version walked on `gotoAndSettle` alone and reported ZERO tables
    on `/malzemeler`, which has one. Same vacuous-measurement shape as the
    storage spec's first version. It now settles the network per route AND
    asserts a per-route floor against a measured census, so a walk that
    measures nothing can no longer pass everything under it.
  · the header sweep targeted `cell.offsetLeft - table.offsetLeft`, which
    assumes cell and table share an offsetParent. On `/kabiliyetler/kalite-
    kontrol`'s three tables they do not; it under-scrolled ~44px and reported
    four reachable columns as lost. It now computes the target from live rects,
    and asks start-reachable and end-reachable separately, because a header
    cell can be WIDER than its port (282.6px in a 278px port at 320) and no
    scroll position frames it whole — WCAG 1.4.10 permits that for tabular
    data and I am not going to fail a table a reader can read.
