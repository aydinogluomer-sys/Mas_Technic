DEFECT R2-1 (BLOCKING) — THE STORAGE TABLE LOSES THREE OF ITS FOUR COLUMNS
BELOW 768 px, AND NOTHING SCROLLS
=============================================================================
The packet asked me to LOOK at the table, because no golden covers the legal
routes (`e2e/visual/wave-b-golden.spec.ts:37` says so deliberately). I did.
Screenshot: shots/cerez-viewport-375.png and shots/cerez-375-after-scroll-attempts.png.

MEASURED at 320 / 375 / 390:
  viewport 375   table width 583.9 px, left edge x=43, right edge 626.9
  header cells   KAYIT [43..256] VISIBLE
                 DEPO  [256..385.2] OFF-SCREEN
                 NE İŞE YARAR [385.2..530.3] OFF-SCREEN
                 SÜRE  [530.3..626.9] OFF-SCREEN
  every one of the five rows shows ONLY its key; the purpose cell and the
  lifetime cell of ALL FIVE records are outside the viewport.
  At 390 `DEPO` becomes visible; columns 3 and 4 never do below 768.
  At 768 and 1280 the table fits and reads correctly.

IT IS NOT MERELY OFF-SCREEN, IT IS UNREACHABLE (table-reach.json):
  · horizontal wheel over the table: window.scrollX stays 0, the fourth
    header's right edge stays at 626.875
  · `scrollLeft = 9999` forced on EVERY ancestor from <table> to
    <documentElement>: every single one stays at 0
  · `window.scrollTo(9999, y)`: scrollX stays 0
  · `.shell-table-scroll` carries NO `tabindex` and NO `role`, so there is no
    keyboard route either
  · `document.documentElement.scrollWidth - clientWidth = 0`
  `div.shell-root` computes `overflow-x: clip`, which — unlike `hidden` — is
  not programmatically scrollable. The columns are gone.

WHY THE REPOSITORY'S OWN GUARDS ARE ALL GREEN ON IT:
  · the reflow assertion measures `scrollWidth - clientWidth <= 1`, which is
    satisfied precisely BECAUSE an ancestor clips (round 1: 15/15 at mobile-320)
  · axe has no rule for content clipped out of a `overflow: clip` ancestor
  · `e2e/visual/wave-b-golden.spec.ts:37` deliberately covers no legal route
  So three independent instruments pass a page that is missing three quarters
  of its most important table on a phone.

IT IS NOT THE FIFTH ROW'S FAULT — measured, in the browser, by hiding the
`mas_intro_seen` row and re-measuring:
  with 5 rows  tableW 583.9  cols [213, 129.2, 145.1, 96.5]
  with 4 rows  tableW 583.9  cols [213, 129.2, 145.1, 96.5]   IDENTICAL
  The row does make the damage far more visible: at 375 it is 246.5 px tall
  (vs 85–126 px for the others) because its long third cell wraps to eight
  invisible lines, leaving a quarter-screen of blank paper in the visible
  strip. But the clip predates C3 and belongs to Phase 08, which created this
  table: `git show 7dcfb65~1:src/pages/CerezPolitikasi.tsx` contains no table
  at all; it arrives in `36c3980`, inside the Phase 08 range.

IT IS UNIQUE TO THIS ONE SURFACE — every other ShellSpecTable behaves
CORRECTLY (table-blastradius.json, 375 px):
  route                                        figure w   scrolls  tabindex
  /blog/havacilik-...malzeme-secimi               333        yes       0
  /kalite-dosyasi                                 333        n/a fits  -
  /hizmetler/cnc-frezeleme  (4 tables)            333        yes       0
  /malzemeler (MaterialRegister)                  333        yes       -
  /cerez-politikasi                             585.9        NO       null

ROOT CAUSE, MEASURED (table-container.json, the containing block of
`figure.shell-table` at 375):
  /blog                 parent div.shell-doc-table                display block, w 333
  /kalite-dosyasi       parent div.shell-span-read shell-stack    grid-template-columns: 333px
  /hizmetler/...        parent div.shell-span-full shell-stack    grid-template-columns: 333px
  /cerez-politikasi     parent div.shell-stack                    grid-template-columns: 585.875px
  `CerezPolitikasi.tsx:133` wraps the table in a BARE `<div className=
  "shell-stack" data-gap="sm">` with no `shell-span-*` class and no
  `shell-doc-table`. `.shell-stack` (shell.css:1098) sets `min-width: 0` on
  ITSELF but its implicit grid track is `auto`, so the track resolves to the
  table's 585.875 px max-content width instead of the 333 px content column.
  The figure then never overflows its wrapper, `.shell-table-scroll`'s
  `overflow-x: auto` (shell.css:1299) never engages, and
  `useScrollableRegionAccess` — which grants the tabindex only
  `if (isScrollable(element))` (useScrollableRegionAccess.ts:160) — grants
  nothing. The escape hatch the primitive's own comment promises ("so a narrow
  viewport scrolls the data", ShellComposition.tsx:203) is inert here.

SMALLEST FIX: give that wrapper the same span class the other document
surfaces use (`shell-span-read` / `shell-span-full`), or put the figure in
`.shell-doc-table` as the journal article does — one class on
`src/pages/CerezPolitikasi.tsx:133`. A `min-width: 0` on `.shell-stack > *`
would fix the class of defect rather than the instance; that is a design call,
not QA's. QA does not make production edits.

WHY IT BLOCKS: Phase 08 criterion 2 requires the legal surfaces to share the
global design system; this is the only one of eight ShellSpecTable surfaces
that does not get the shell's table behaviour. The mandatory task list names
"tables" explicitly. And the document's central completeness claim — "hepsi
02. maddede listelenmiştir" — points a mobile reader at a table whose purpose
and lifetime columns they cannot read by any means.
