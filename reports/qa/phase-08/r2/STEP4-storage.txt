holds a hardcoded `HCAPTCHA_SITE_KEY`, `:230` renders `<HCaptcha>` inside the
form. It mounts with the page — no interaction is required to make the
requests or set the cookie.

`/giris` IS A PUBLIC PAGE. The repository's own contract says so:
`e2e/shared-shell-accessibility.spec.ts` names it in `NON_SHELL_PUBLIC_ROUTES`.
