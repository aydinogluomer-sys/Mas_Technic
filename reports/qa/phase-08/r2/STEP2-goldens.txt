WHY 1280/1440 LOST TWO PITCHES AND 768 LOST ONE — measured, not assumed
`.tl-footer nav` computed style:
    1280 / 1440   grid-template-rows: 150px          one row, four across
    768           grid-template-rows: 150px 150px    2x2, two distinct row Ys
                  (559.8 and 733.8), columns at x=57 and x=412
  1280/1440 band = tallest of all four columns: 8 -> 6 rows = -2 x 22.50 = -45 px.
  768 band = (tallest of row 1) + (tallest of row 2):
      before  row1 max(HIZ 5, KAB 5) = 5   row2 max(END 5, KUR 8) = 8   = 13
      after   row1 max(HIZ 5, KAB 6) = 6   row2 max(END 5, KUR 6) = 6   = 12
      net -1 x 22.50 = -22.5 px  ->  the measured -22 / -23 px.
  The Coder's "two pitches at 1280/1440 and one at 768" is confirmed, and the
  mechanism is the 2x2 grid. That mechanism is NOT in the footer-groups.ts
  comment, which asserts a single "tallest column" model that only holds at
  >=1024.
