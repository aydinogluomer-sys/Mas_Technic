# Independent scorecard

Weights:

- Visual design and hierarchy: 25%
- Usability and orientation: 25%
- Originality and craft: 15%
- Content clarity: 15%
- Navigation architecture: 20%

Blockers override score:

- Overall below 90 or usability below 90.
- Serious/critical axe violation.
- Broken focus trap, Escape, restore, or background inertness.
- Missing route or data-count drift from 3 families / 15 categories / 48 details.
- Clipped primary content or unreachable CTA in a target viewport.
- Reduced-motion path still uses stagger, parallax, clip reveal, or large translation.

Support every score with captured/rendered evidence and list no more than five highest-leverage fixes.
