# Art-direction contract

- Palette: near-black graphite, warm off-white, controlled copper/amber metal accents.
- Grid: 12 columns desktop; 3–4 family, 5–8 categories, 9–12 technical preview.
- Type: oversized indexed family names; uppercase micro-labels only for metadata.
- Graphics: datum lines, ticks, coordinates, tolerances, and restrained machining contours.
- Surfaces: use borders, gradients, grain, and specular strips; avoid glassmorphism and heavy blur.
- Hierarchy: selected state must remain obvious without motion or color alone.
- Density: preserve breathing room around primary choices; technical data may be dense but secondary.
- Mobile: prefer clarity and reachability over reproducing desktop spectacle.
